the outermost tag that matches a node
wins and draws one line. if Cin Auravel
has no node yet, Cin and Auravel get
their lines instead.

matching is exact. case, spaces, all of it.
a tag with no node waits, dormant, until
you make one with that exact name.

aliases are extra exact names for a node.
[Cinnabar] links to Cinren Auravel once
you add Cinnabar as its alias.
